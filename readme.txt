Lightweight recurring subscriptions for WooCommerce with automatic Stripe and Square renewals.

== Description ==

Turn any simple or variable product into a subscription. Customers are charged automatically each period through the official Stripe or Square plugins. Any other gateway gets an emailed invoice with a pay link. Failed payments are retried, and customers can pause, resume, cancel or change their card from My Account.

See README.md for setup, how it works and its limits.
